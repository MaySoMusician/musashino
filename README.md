# musashino

**musashino** is a private software suite that enables you to host the Japanese-specific Small Language Model [**sarashina**](https://huggingface.co/collections/sbintuitions/sarashina22-67c65fdab1ff63d92defb47e) behind NAT and firewalls, while still safely making it publicly accessible over the Internet.

## Origin of the Name

The name *musashino* traces its origin to the location of SB Intuitions—developer of *sarashina*—namely Takeshiba, as well as to the *Takeshiba Legend* mentioned in the *Sarashina Diary*. (This is one of the oldest Japanese records in which the place name Takeshiba appears.)

In short: According to legend, along with a few written texts and oral transmissions, during the early Heian period, Imperial guards, overwhelmed by their harsh working conditions, fled the palace, and in doing so, abducted a Female Imperial Princess. After a lengthy journey, a single surviving guard returned with Her Highness to his hometown of Takeshiba (known in those days as Musashi Province). Various political struggles and twists ensued until the kidnapping was exposed; however, His Majesty the Emperor at that time ultimately granted permission for Her Highness to remain in Takeshiba, where She had grown fond of both the guard and the land. Although even the Emperor could not permit them a formal marriage, their child was graciously bestowed the surname *Musashi*.

As for Her Highness’s palace, it eventually became a temple, which housed a manuscript detailing these events. Perhaps due to the inconvenience posed by such irregular happenings, later authorities seized the opportunity presented by ongoing warfare to loot and destroy the original text. The tale that survives widely in the present world is no more than a fairy-tale version. In truth, it appears that the temple’s chief priest reconstructed the story from memory to the best of his ability and preserved it through oral tradition in a modest manner. Having become acquainted with these circumstances, I deemed it fitting to connect the name *musashino* with the offering that bears the legend-inspired name *sarashina*, and thus christened these software components accordingly.
